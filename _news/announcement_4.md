Our paper has been accepted and is now available online in **Knowledge-Based Systems**:
Phan Tai Duc; Nhut Minh Nguyen; Khang Phuc Nguyen; Phuong-Nam Tran; Nhat Truong Pham; Linh Le; Choong Seon Hong; **Duc Ngoc Minh Dang**, 
[From object difficulty to image scoring: A strategy for active learning in object detection](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.knosys.2026.115946), 2026.
This work proposes a novel strategy to improve active learning in object detection by effectively aggregating object-level difficulty into image-level scoring.
